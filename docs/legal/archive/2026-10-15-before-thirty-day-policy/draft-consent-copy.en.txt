1. Registration notice proposal

Text: Read our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to understand your account and how Pickle handles your information.

Proposed terms checkbox, initially unchecked: I agree to the Terms of Service.

Decision note:
Providing a privacy notice, acknowledging receipt, agreeing to service terms and consenting to a particular use of personal information are different actions. Do not use a blanket 'I consent to all data processing' checkbox. Which processing requires a separate consent and its items must be determined from the actual purposes and applicable law. Registration acceptance recording is not implemented merely by writing this file. Social sign-in users require the same applicable notice/agreement flow; provider login alone is not a Pickle terms agreement record.

1. Who operates Pickle and how to contact us
Pickle is operated by Taelim Auh, based in the Republic of Korea; admin@getpickleapp.com. For privacy questions and requests, contact admin@getpickleapp.com. This policy covers the Pickle app and its related services.

2. Account and profile information
For email registration, we process your email address, authentication credentials through our authentication provider, account identifier, username and display name. If you use an enabled social login, we also process the identity and profile information provided through that sign-in flow. Google sign-in is currently enabled; Apple sign-in is planned but not offered yet. Confirm actual Google-returned field scope against the provider configuration. You may supply a profile photo and a short biography. Username and display name are currently fixed after selection; other supported profile fields can be managed through the app. Passwords are handled by the authentication service, not published with your profile.

3. Content, community activity and messages
We process recipes and drafts, ingredients, cooking instructions, tags, linked videos, uploaded images, comments, replies and ratings/reviews that you submit. We also process saved recipes, likes, follow relationships, author-notification preferences, user blocks, and in-app notifications to provide those features.
Direct messages include the sender, recipient, message text, optional photo, creation time and read status. Messages are stored by our service to deliver them and provide conversation history. They are not public posts: ordinary in-app access is limited to the participants. This does not mean messages are end-to-end encrypted. Each participant can copy or disclose what they receive. Necessary service administration, security handling and valid legal requests may involve access by authorised personnel under the applicable process.

4. Search, recipe views, device storage and technical data
Submitted general searches are logged with the search text, normalised text, result count, time and account identifier when signed in. Recipe-view records store a recipe identifier and time, rather than an account identifier in the current recipe_views table. We use these records for discovery, popularity counts and service statistics. The current recommendation function can display frequently submitted search terms to other users without identifying the searcher; do not enter personal or sensitive details in search fields. The use of a 30-day recommendation window does not itself delete older search events.
The app stores a recent-search list, recipe draft data, theme/settings and authentication/session information on the device. Some search history is device-scoped rather than account-scoped. Removing a recent term from the device does not erase server search-event records. Service providers may process network addresses, request/device/browser information and operational logs necessary to deliver and secure the service. 5. Reports, enforcement and policy agreement records
We process reports, reporting and reported account identifiers, reasons, descriptions and snapshots of reported content. We retain moderation cases, assigned staff identifiers, decisions, public reasons, internal notes, timestamps, restrictions and appeals to review abuse, explain actions and handle challenges. Authors are not given reporter identities or internal administrator notes through moderation notices. Reporters receive a limited completion result.
When you agree before sharing content, we store your account identifier, the accepted terms/content-policy version and the server acceptance time. Current consent records represent agreement to the Terms of Service and Content Policy; they are not a record of optional advertising consent or blanket permission for unrelated processing.

We process information to create and secure accounts, deliver app features and conversations, publish the content you choose to share, maintain your collections and social connections, provide search/discovery and statistics, send requested/service notices, address support requests, prevent abuse and handle policy enforcement and appeals. 7. What other users can see
Published recipes, recipe photos, usernames, display names, profile photos/biographies, comments, reviews and visible social/activity counts may be accessible to other users, including visitors where the service allows it. Drafts and private messages are not intentionally published as public recipes. Public profile/recipe images use public file URLs; hiding a screen or blocking an account cannot recall a URL or a copy already obtained. Users can also share public links and capture content outside the service.

8. Service providers and international processing
Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage and server functions. The current primary project database region is ap-northeast-2 (Seoul); this is not a guarantee that every supporting service, log or backup remains in Korea.
Resend is used for service email delivery. Authentication messages are sent through the configured authentication/SMTP flow. Moderation email delivery sends the recipient email address and public notice content, which can include the content type, recipe title, action, reason and restriction duration. Reporter identities and internal moderator notes are excluded from those email payloads.
Vercel hosts the web service. Porkbun forwards inquiries to admin@getpickleapp.com to the operator’s existing Gmail mailbox. Google login is enabled; Apple login is planned. [CONFIRM PROVIDER PROCESSING COUNTRIES, TRANSFER ARRANGEMENTS AND RETENTION.]
When you play an embedded third-party video or open an external link, the destination provider can process information such as your network address and playback/request data under its own policies. The player supports external video sources; only enabled/used providers should be listed in the final notice.
We may disclose information when you direct us to share it or where necessary and lawful to respond to valid requests, protect people or secure the service. [CONFIRM ANY ADDITIONAL BUSINESS DISCLOSURES.]

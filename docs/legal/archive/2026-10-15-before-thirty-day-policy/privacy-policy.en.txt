1. Information we collect
We process your email and authentication information through Supabase, account ID, username, display name, optional profile photo and biography; recipes and drafts, photos, comments, replies, reviews and message text/photos; saves, likes, follows, blocks and notification preferences. We also process reports, content snapshots, moderation decisions and appeals, policy-agreement versions/times, search events and recipe-view counts. Birth dates entered for account eligibility are used to evaluate the 18+ rule without storing a birth-date field; the server keeps the adult self-declaration, rule version and confirmation time. Service providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. Purposes for collecting and using information
We use account, authentication and 18+ eligibility records to register, sign in, secure and activate accounts. We use profile details, recipes, drafts, uploaded images, comments and reviews to provide the publishing and community features you choose. Message text/photos, participant IDs and read status provide private delivery and conversation history. Saves, likes, follows, blocks and notification choices provide your collections, social connections, safety controls and requested alerts. Search and recipe-view records support discovery, recommendations and usage statistics. Reports, evidence, moderation decisions and appeals support abuse prevention, policy enforcement and review. Agreement versions/times document your choices. Contact details and inquiry contents let us respond to support/privacy requests and send authentication or service notices.

3. Service providers
Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage and server functions and processes the account/content data needed for those features; the current primary database region is Seoul, South Korea. Vercel hosts and delivers the web service and may process web-request technical information. Resend receives recipient email addresses and message content to deliver authentication and service/moderation emails. Porkbun forwards inquiries sent to admin@getpickleapp.com, including sender details and inquiry contents, to the operator’s Gmail mailbox provided by Google. Google also provides the currently enabled social sign-in flow and related identity data. Apple sign-in is planned but is not currently offered. Provider infrastructure, support, logs or delivery may involve processing outside South Korea; the database region does not determine every provider’s processing location. External video playback and links contact their respective providers under their own data practices.

4. Sharing and sale of information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate Pickle, when you direct us to share it, or when disclosure is required to comply with law, protect users, or protect the rights and security of Pickle.

5. Public and private information
Other users or visitors, where the feature permits, can see your username/tag, display name, profile photo, biography, published recipe titles/descriptions/ingredients/steps/photos/video links, comments/replies and review/rating content. Public activity includes visible post, follower/following, recipe-view and like counts. Frequently searched terms may also appear as recommendations without naming the searcher, as explained in section 11. Your email address, authentication credentials, private drafts, saved-recipe collection, message contents/photos, block list and private report/appeal submissions are not intended for public display. Ordinary message/photo access is limited to the participants. Authorised staff may access information needed for service administration, security, moderation or valid legal requests; this is not an end-to-end encrypted messaging service. Participants can copy received content. Public image URLs and copies already obtained cannot be recalled merely by hiding a screen or blocking a user.

6. Retention, deletion and retained records
We retain account records and user content to provide the account and features you use. You can delete supported content in the app and request account deletion through Profile settings > Delete Account, or ask admin@getpickleapp.com for help. Completed account deletion removes your account, published recipes, drafts, uploaded account/recipe/chat photos and linked activity such as saves, likes, follows and blocks. Messages involving the deleted account are removed from the service conversation records. Uploaded files are removed through the storage service; the app confirms completion only after the account-deletion process succeeds. If a step fails, some files may already be removed, new ordinary writes are restricted, and deletion must be retried or supported by the operator. Some reports, content snapshots, moderation decisions/audit records and search events can remain after account references are removed; text or other identifiers may still contain personal information. Retention needs must be assessed against the specific safety review, appeal, actual dispute or applicable legal obligation rather than an unspecified possible future use. Provider logs, email copies and backups have separate lifecycles, and their exact retention periods have not yet been verified. Fixed automatic expiry periods for retained categories have not yet been configured. The 30-day search-recommendation and 90-day repeat-violation windows are not deletion schedules. Content hiding/public removal is also different from physical file or evidence erasure. These records and existing copies are not all erased immediately by account deletion. Contact admin@getpickleapp.com to request review/deletion of retained information, subject to a justified applicable retention requirement.

7. Your choices
You can manage supported profile fields, collections, follows, author alerts and blocks, edit/delete your supported content, and request account deletion in Settings. Username and display name are currently fixed in the ordinary UI. You may contact admin@getpickleapp.com to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion or other data rights; we may verify your identity proportionately. Device search history, theme, drafts and authentication/session data are stored locally. Removing a recent search from the device does not delete its server search event. On successful app account deletion we attempt to clear this account’s draft/settings cache and shared recent searches on that device; other devices or OS backups can retain copies.

8. Children
Pickle accounts are available only to people aged 18 or over. Existing users and Google sign-in users must also complete the account eligibility check before activating ordinary account features. Public recipe browsing is separate from account activation; the service is not directed to children. Age entry is a self-declaration, not government-ID verification. A minimal authentication record may exist before the post-login check, especially with social sign-in. If you are under 18, do not activate an account; sign out or use account deletion/support. Contact us if you believe an underage account exists.

9. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as Pickle changes. When we make material changes, we will update the date shown on this page and provide additional notice when appropriate.

Use this address for questions about data collection, public visibility, service providers or retention, and for applicable access, correction, deletion or account-deletion assistance requests. We may need proportionate identity verification to handle a request. Initial store distribution is planned for the Philippines; future country availability will be introduced separately.

11. Search and discovery records
Submitted general search events include the search text, normalised text, result count, time and account ID when signed in. Frequently searched terms may be shown to other users without naming the searcher, so do not enter private or sensitive information in search fields. The recommendation query uses a recent 30-day window; that window is not an automatic deletion schedule. Recipe-view rows contain a recipe ID and time, not an account ID in that table. Provider request logs are separate.

12. Moderation notices and service email
Reports, content snapshots, internal evidence and appeal records are used for safety review and oversight with access restricted to authorised staff. Authors receive public reasons, clauses, actions and restriction periods; they are not given reporter identities or internal notes through those notices. Email delivery can send Resend the recipient address, public notice text and recipe title. This is separate from marketing consent. The 90-day repeat-violation assessment window is not a record-deletion period.

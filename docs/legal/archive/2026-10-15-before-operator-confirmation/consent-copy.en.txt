ACCOUNT DELETION DIALOG
Heading: Delete your account?
Body: This permanently deletes your profile, saved recipes, likes, comments, and account. This action cannot be undone.

Important audit observation (not user-facing copy):
The current deletion function removes avatar/chat photos before requesting auth account deletion. Published recipes refer to profiles without an ON DELETE cleanup rule, and the inspected schema has no corresponding deletion trigger. Deleting an account that owns published recipes may fail after some photos were already removed. Do not extend this copy to promise deletion of every file, report or backup before the deletion implementation and retention decisions are aligned.

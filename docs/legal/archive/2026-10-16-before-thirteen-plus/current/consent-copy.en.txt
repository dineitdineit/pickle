ACCOUNT DELETION IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
The server returns accepted and completed separately, including an account-matched job reference. Clients accept only a matching confirmed queued request or completed deletion. A one-time session notice shows the reference/status after sign-out; its minimal local record expires after 30 days and is consumed when displayed. Confirmation does not contain an email or message body.
The durable manifest and expiring worker leases support automatic bounded retries. Core photos/records are deleted rather than copied into a recovery backup. After completion a minimal encrypted receipt is backed up in private Storage, verified by download/hash, and expires after 30 days. Storage deletion must succeed before its ledger is removed. Detailed closed-case evidence expires after 30 days; minimum enforcement history after 90 days, with ongoing case/appeal/active-restriction exceptions. Provider-managed backups/logs/mailboxes remain separate.

1. Information we collect
We process your email and authentication information through Supabase, account ID, username, display name, optional profile photo and biography; recipes and drafts, photo posts and captions, photos, comments, replies, reviews and message text/photos; saves, likes, follows, blocks and notification preferences. We also process reports, content snapshots, moderation decisions and appeals, policy-agreement versions/times, search events, recipe-view counts and minimal deletion-job/receipt data including identifiers, cleanup paths, timestamps and sanitized status codes. Birth dates entered for account eligibility are used to evaluate the 18+ rule without storing a birth-date field; the server keeps the adult self-declaration, rule version and confirmation time. Service providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. Purposes for collecting and using information
We use account, authentication and 18+ eligibility records to register, sign in, secure and activate accounts. We use profile details, recipes, drafts, photo posts and captions, uploaded images, comments and reviews to provide the publishing and community features you choose. Message text/photos, participant IDs and read status provide private delivery and conversation history. Saves, likes, follows, blocks and notification choices provide your collections, social connections, safety controls and requested alerts. Search and recipe-view records support discovery, recommendations and usage statistics. Reports, evidence, moderation decisions and appeals support abuse prevention, policy enforcement and review. Agreement versions/times document your choices. Contact details and inquiry contents let us respond to support/privacy requests and send authentication or service notices.

3. Service providers
Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage and server functions and processes the account/content data needed for those features; the current primary database region is Seoul, South Korea. Vercel hosts and delivers the web service and may process web-request technical information. Resend receives recipient email addresses and message content to deliver authentication and service/moderation emails. Porkbun forwards inquiries sent to admin@getpickleapp.com, including sender details and inquiry contents, to the operator’s Gmail mailbox provided by Google. Google also provides the currently enabled social sign-in flow and related identity data. Apple sign-in is planned but is not currently offered. Provider infrastructure, support, logs or delivery may involve processing outside South Korea; the database region does not determine every provider’s processing location. External video playback and links contact their respective providers under their own data practices. Pickle uses the Supabase JavaScript SDK for authentication, database, storage and realtime requests, and Capacitor App/Browser for app and browser integration. We have not integrated a dedicated advertising, external analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK in this app build. Provider request logs may include technical request information such as IP address, user agent, request path, time, status and account identifiers where applicable; actual fields and retention depend on the provider and configuration.

4. Sharing and sale of information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate Pickle, when you direct us to share it, or when disclosure is required to comply with law, protect users, or protect the rights and security of Pickle.

5. Public and private information
Other users or visitors, where the feature permits, can see your username/tag, display name, profile photo, biography, published recipe titles/descriptions/ingredients/steps/photos/video links, published photo-post photos and captions, comments/replies and review/rating content. Public activity includes visible post, follower/following, recipe-view and like counts. Frequently searched terms may also appear as recommendations without naming the searcher, as explained in section 11. Your email address, authentication credentials, private drafts, saved-recipe collection, message contents/photos, block list and private report/appeal submissions are not intended for public display. Ordinary message/photo access is limited to the participants. Authorised staff may access information needed for service administration, security, moderation or valid legal requests; this is not an end-to-end encrypted messaging service. Participants can copy received content. User-uploaded recipe and post images are stored privately; draft previews are limited to the owner and authorised staff, and published recipe and post images can be accessed through short-lived URLs after a visibility check. Profile photos and legacy catalogue images remain public. URLs already issued, cached files and copies already obtained cannot all be recalled immediately by hiding a screen or blocking a user.

6. Retention, deletion and retained records
Deleting a photo post or deleting/replacing a recipe/draft queues unused user-uploaded recipe/post images for Storage deletion with retries; images still referenced by saved content or retained report evidence remain subject to their applicable retention rules. Unsaved uploads abandoned without an explicit discard are eligible for cleanup after seven days. Unpublished server post drafts are eligible for cleanup after seven days without an update; selected photos and edits are held only in memory until upload and are lost when the composer is closed. Account records and content remain available while needed to provide your account and the features you use; the 30-day rule is not automatic deletion of an active account. Request deletion through Profile settings > Delete Account or admin@getpickleapp.com. We immediately attempt removal of your account, published recipes and photo posts, drafts, uploaded account/recipe/post/chat photos, messages and linked activity. Your profile and content are hidden and new ordinary writes are restricted while cleanup runs. File or authentication failures are queued for automatic retry; an accepted request is not falsely described as completed deletion. A minimal deletion job keeps the account identifier, file-cleanup paths, timestamps and sanitized status/error codes while needed to finish the request. After successful deletion, that minimal receipt and its application-managed encrypted backup expire in 30 days. We do not create a recovery copy of your recipes, photo posts, messages or photos in that backup. Detailed report snapshots, evidence and notes expire 30 days after case closure or final appeal review, whichever is later. Minimum enforcement history is retained for 90 days; an open case/appeal and the minimum state needed to enforce an active restriction remain until resolved or the restriction ends. Search events and in-app moderation, recipe-comment and comment-reply notices expire after 30 days; the application email queue expires 30 days after sending or, if not sent, creation. Non-identifying recipe statistics are separate from personal search records. Cleanup is scheduled automatically; failed physical deletion remains pending, is retried and is visible to the operator. Expiry is a processing deadline, not a guarantee that a provider outage cannot delay erasure. Vendor-managed logs, mailbox copies and infrastructure backups are outside this application-managed archive; their actual lifecycles require separate provider settings and are not claimed to have been changed by our 30-day job. Public copies already obtained and other-device caches cannot all be recalled. Contact admin@getpickleapp.com for deletion assistance or a review of retained information.

7. Your choices
You can manage supported profile fields, collections, follows, author alerts and blocks, edit/delete your supported content, and request account deletion in Settings. Username and display name are currently fixed in the ordinary UI. You may contact admin@getpickleapp.com to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion or other data rights; we may verify your identity proportionately. Device search history, theme, drafts and Supabase authentication/session data are stored locally in browser/WebView storage. Recent searches are cleared when signing out or changing accounts, and can persist for the same account on that device. This Android build excludes its local app data from OS cloud backup and device transfer using backup rules; previously created backups and copies on other devices are not recalled by these settings. Newly uploaded profile, recipe, post and chat photos are decoded and re-encoded on the device before transfer to avoid forwarding source EXIF/GPS and other embedded metadata; this does not describe a retrospective rewrite of older uploads. Removing a recent search from the device does not delete its server search event. After an accepted app account-deletion request we attempt to clear this account’s draft/settings cache and shared recent searches on that device; other devices or OS backups can retain copies.

8. Children
Pickle accounts are available only to people aged 18 or over. Existing users and Google sign-in users must also complete the account eligibility check before activating ordinary account features. Public recipe browsing is separate from account activation; the service is not directed to children. Age entry is a self-declaration, not government-ID verification. A minimal authentication record may exist before the post-login check, especially with social sign-in. If you are under 18, do not activate an account; sign out or use account deletion/support. Contact us if you believe an underage account exists.

9. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as Pickle changes. When we make material changes, we will update the date shown on this page and provide additional notice when appropriate.

11. Search and discovery records
Submitted general search events include the search text, normalised text, result count, time and account ID when signed in. Recommended search terms are limited to terms matching public ingredient/tag names, containing only permitted word characters and searched by at least five distinct signed-in accounts in the recent 30-day window. Searcher identities are not returned in recommendations; do not enter private or sensitive information in search fields. Raw search events expire after 30 days and are removed by the scheduled retention task. Recipe-view rows contain a recipe ID and time, not an account ID in that table; ordinary clients receive aggregate counts rather than raw viewing timestamps. Recipe statistics have a separate service purpose. A follower’s author-alert preference is available through their own account controls rather than direct relationship-table reads. Provider request logs are separate.

12. Moderation notices and service email
Reports, content snapshots, evidence and appeal records are used for safety review and oversight with access restricted to authorised staff. Detailed evidence is removed 30 days after final case/appeal closure; minimum enforcement history is retained for 90 days, subject to an ongoing case/appeal or active restriction as described in section 6. Authors receive public reasons, clauses, actions and restriction periods; reporter identities and internal notes are not disclosed through those notices. Moderation notices in the app expire after 30 days. Resend can receive the recipient address, public notice text and recipe title. The app email queue is cleared after 30 days; this does not delete a copy already delivered to a mailbox or set Resend’s own retention. These service messages are separate from marketing consent. Comments on your recipes and replies to your comments also generate in-app alerts using participant, recipe and comment identifiers, without a separate copy of the comment body. These comment/reply alerts expire after 30 days and are hidden when their content is unavailable or the relevant users are blocked. This version does not send comment/reply emails or device push notifications.
